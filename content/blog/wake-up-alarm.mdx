## The real bug is the morning

Most alarms solve the narrow problem of producing sound at a certain time. The
person who set the alarm still has to become awake, make a decision, and start
moving.

I want to build a personal wake-up alarm that treats the morning as a system:
light, sound, motion, friction, and a small amount of accountability working
together.

## The design question

How much intervention is useful before the device becomes annoying enough to
defeat itself?

That makes this a hardware project and a product project at the same time. The
interesting part is not adding more features. It is finding the smallest loop
that reliably changes what happens after the alarm rings.
