Most of my work has lived in separate places: a product repository, a resume,
class notes, hardware experiments, reading highlights, and conversations about
what I want to build next.

This site is an attempt to connect those pieces without pretending they are
finished.

## A record of direction

I am interested in artificial intelligence, evidence systems, embedded
engineering, computer vision, physics, and astrophysics. Those subjects can
look unrelated when they are listed together. To me, they share a central
question:

> How do we observe a complex system well enough to understand it and act
> responsibly?

Building Elev made that question practical. Studying physics makes it deeper.
Working with hardware makes it impossible to hide from the details.

## What belongs here

The site will hold essays, reading notes, and an account of the questions I am
working through. I want it to show the development of the work, not only the
polished endpoint.

The standard is simple: publish what is useful, keep private what is not mine to
share, and update the record when the evidence changes.
