## The question

Mission-driven teams do a great deal of valuable work, but the evidence needed
for grants, reporting, and program improvement is often scattered across forms,
documents, staff knowledge, and field activity.

Elev explores a practical question: how can software help a program collect the
right evidence without turning the work into more administrative burden?

## My role

As co-founder and CEO, I have worked across product strategy, customer
discovery, sales, system architecture, and implementation. The platform brings
together a React and TypeScript interface, service APIs, retrieval workflows,
structured data, and source citations.

## Engineering priorities

- Keep generated findings connected to their underlying sources.
- Protect personal information as evidence moves through the system.
- Make collection plans understandable to the people doing the work.
- Support both structured program data and unstructured field submissions.

The project continues to evolve through real deployment feedback. The goal is
not to automate judgment. It is to give people better evidence for making it.
