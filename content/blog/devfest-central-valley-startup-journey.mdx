## A startup story in a developer room

At Google Developer Groups DevFest Central Valley 2025, I spoke about a
startup journey in the Central Valley. The useful part of the talk was not
turning the story into a clean success narrative. It was making the sequence
of decisions, uncertainty, and work visible to other builders.

## Why speak about it

Founder stories often become too polished after the fact. A local developer
conference is a better place to talk about the awkward middle: learning while
building, finding people who will challenge the plan, and turning a regional
starting point into an advantage rather than an apology.

This note will hold the lessons I want to keep from that conversation and from
the people I met around it.
