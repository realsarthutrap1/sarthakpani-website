## Before the category

Before I knew to call it entrepreneurship, I was making small things and
trying to find out whether anyone else found them useful.

The early sites were not polished startups. They were experiments in taste,
copy, sales, distribution, and the uncomfortable distance between an idea and
someone willing to use it.

## What stayed with me

The useful lesson was not that every experiment worked. It was that building
in public makes vague ambition answerable. A page has to explain itself. A
product has to earn a second visit. An offer has to make sense to someone who
does not share the founder's excitement.

This is a record of those first attempts, including the parts that looked small
at the time and turned out to be the beginning of a pattern.
