## From command to motion

This prototype connected an ESP32 and Raspberry Pi over a Wi-Fi and UDP control
link. The embedded side handled L298N motor driving and PWM-based motion
control, while the browser-facing side exposed live camera feedback and image
controls.

## What it taught me

Physical systems force software assumptions into the open. Latency becomes
visible. Timing matters. A small inconsistency in power, networking, or motor
control can become the entire behavior of the machine.

The project strengthened my understanding of:

- Embedded networking and message design
- PWM motor control
- Camera streaming on constrained hardware
- Debugging across hardware, firmware, and browser boundaries

It also reinforced a broader principle: useful intelligence needs a reliable
way to sense and act in the world.
