## Learning to see computationally

These prototypes explored how software can extract identity, position, and
motion from camera input. They included facial recognition, real-time object
detection and tracking, and an early boxing-coach concept based on body
landmarks.

## Feedback from motion

The boxing prototype used pose landmarks and movement analysis to examine form
and technique. Its purpose was not to replace a coach, but to test whether
computer vision could make practice more observable.

The experiments introduced the full loop behind applied vision: capture,
preprocess, infer, interpret, and communicate. Each stage can change whether the
result is genuinely useful.
